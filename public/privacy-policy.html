Keystone CRM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Keystone CRM ("Keystone", "the Service", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when you use our customer relationship management platform for real-estate brokerages, including when you connect third-party accounts such as Google or Microsoft.

Keystone CRM is provided by Red Brick Smart Systems and is deployed for participating brokerages. For a given brokerage's CRM data, that brokerage is the data controller and Red Brick Smart Systems acts as the data processor. This policy applies to the Service and to our website at crm.pristineproperties.ae.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details — name, work email address, phone number, role, profile photo and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • CRM content — leads, buyers, deals, tasks, meetings, showings, notes, property/listing records, photos and documents that you or your brokerage enter into the Service.
  • Communications — messages you send to us for support.

Information from connected accounts

If you choose to connect a Google or Microsoft account, we access only the data needed for the features you enable:

ConnectionData accessedPurpose
Calendar (Google Calendar / Microsoft Outlook) Basic profile (name, email address); ability to create, update and delete calendar events on your primary calendar Sync your CRM viewings, meetings, tasks and reminders to your own calendar
Cloud storage (Google Drive / Microsoft OneDrive) Basic profile (name, email address); files created by the Service in an app-specific area Store inventory and listing photos you upload through the Service

We do not request or access the contents of your email, your contacts, or any Google/Microsoft data beyond the scopes listed above. We do not read calendar events that were not created or managed by the Service.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and log data — IP address, browser/device type, pages viewed, timestamps and actions taken, used for security, diagnostics and improving the Service.
  • Cookies — essential session cookies for authentication and security. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, maintain and secure the Service.
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts, roles and permissions.
  • Sync CRM records with calendars and cloud storage you explicitly connect.
  • Send transactional and reminder notifications (in-app, email, calendar).
  • Provide support and respond to requests.
  • Monitor for abuse, fraud, and security incidents, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Google API Services — Limited Use Disclosure

Keystone's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, and consistent with the Limited Use requirements:

  • We use Google user data only to provide or improve the user-facing features described in this policy.
  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for advertising, ad targeting, or for training generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data, except: with your explicit consent for specific support requests; where necessary for security purposes (such as investigating abuse); to comply with applicable law; or where the data has been aggregated and anonymized.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary for the features above, to comply with law, or as part of a merger/acquisition with notice to you.

4. How We Share Information

  • Within your brokerage — CRM data is visible to authorized users of your brokerage according to their roles and permissions.
  • Service providers — hosting, email delivery and similar vendors who process data on our behalf under contract and only as needed to operate the Service.
  • Connected providers — Google and Microsoft, strictly to perform the sync you enabled.
  • Legal and safety — when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as the account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. CRM content is retained according to your brokerage's instructions. Access and refresh tokens for connected accounts are retained until you disconnect the account or delete your account, after which they are deleted from active systems.

6. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), encryption at rest for stored OAuth tokens, role-based access controls, audit logging and regular backups. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Transfers

Information may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country. Where required, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access, correction and deletion — you can access and update most information in the Service, or contact us to request a copy or deletion of your personal information.
  • Disconnecting connected accounts — you can disconnect Google or Microsoft at any time from My Cloud in the Service. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens for that connection and stops future syncing.
  • Revoking provider access — you can also revoke access from your Google Account permissions or your Microsoft account.
  • Notifications — you can manage reminder preferences in the Service.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or UAE data-protection laws. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. If your request concerns data entered by your brokerage, we may refer you to that brokerage as the controller.

9. Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated in the Service.
